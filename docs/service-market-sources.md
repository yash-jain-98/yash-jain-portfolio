# Service-page market context

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Content lives in `scripts/service-insights.mjs` and is rendered as static HTML on the four service pages. Each metric shows its market, period, definition and original source. These are external statistics, not portfolio outcomes or guarantees. The Australian SME and OECD surveys use different populations and definitions; do not compare their percentages as equivalent adoption rates. WEF outlook figures are employer expectations rather than observed outcomes.

Sources verified:

- National AI Centre, SME AI Pulse, December 2025–February 2026; article first published 7 May 2026, updated 3 June: https://www.ai.gov.au/news-and-insights/blog/ai-adoption-insights-december-2025-february-2026
- OECD AI topic page, business adoption context for 2025: https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/artificial-intelligence.html
- Australia Post, eCommerce Report 2026, full-year 2025 spending data attributed to CommBank iQ: https://auspost.com.au/business/grow-your-business/2026-ecommerce-report
- Eurostat, Digital economy and society statistics — enterprises, data extracted January 2026, PDF pages 9–10. Survey coverage is selected economic sectors with at least ten employees/self-employed persons: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/33473.pdf
- World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs Report 2025, published 7 January 2025. Global employer survey and outlook through 2030: https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025

Design reference: https://birdeye.com/au/jay-coworker/. Its client-performance numbers have not been reused. Feature copy describes capabilities that can be scoped; it adds no invented client results, certifications or numerical performance promises. Geographic findings are labelled individually and not presented as Indian market estimates. Review these sources when updating the service catalogue or when newer comparable datasets are published.
