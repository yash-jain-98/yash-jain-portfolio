03 / LEAD OPERATIONS

New form entries.
Matched and moved.

A Google Apps Script workflow that checks each new Google Forms response against two other Google Sheets. When a matching record exists, the entry moves to the “Convert” sheet.

  • Google Forms
  • Google Sheets
  • Google Apps Script
  1. 01Receive entryA form response enters the sheet
  2. 02Check two sheetsLook for an existing matching record
  3. 03Move the matchSend the entry to the Convert sheet
Inside the workflow +

The taskCross-checking new leads against existing records across multiple sheets.

The automationWhen a Google Forms response adds a row, the script checks for the data in two other Google Sheets. If it finds a match, it moves the entry to a sheet named Convert.

The resultMatching entries are organised in a dedicated sheet for the lead-conversion workflow.

What kinds of projects can we work on?+

Business websites, landing pages, interface improvements, Power BI and Excel dashboards, SQL analysis, data-science studies, and focused Python automations. Share your idea and we can work out the right scope.

Can you improve something I already have?+

Yes. We can start with an existing website, report, dataset, or workflow, identify the friction, and agree on the changes that would be most useful.

How do pricing and timelines work?+

They depend on the scope, complexity, and available materials. After discussing your goals, I’ll propose deliverables, a timeline, and pricing for you to review before work begins.

What should I include in my first message?+

A short description of your idea, links to anything you already have, your preferred timeline, and an approximate budget if you have one. It’s fine if you are still figuring out the details.
