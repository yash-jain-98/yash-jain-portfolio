Stroke prediction & model evaluation

The problem
Can health-record classifiers identify stroke cases in an imbalanced dataset?
My contribution
Prepared the data, compared four classifiers and reviewed recall alongside accuracy.
The result
The recorded tree found 11 of 62 stroke cases; three models found none. This is an educational study, not a medical tool.
Pythonscikit-learnClassificationModel evaluation
Explore project

An educational study of 5,110 records from the Kaggle Stroke Prediction Dataset. The notebook compares logistic regression, SVM, a decision tree and KNN. The README corrects count-versus-rate interpretations and reviews class imbalance, encoding, missing BMI values and evaluation limitations. It is not a medical tool.

A closer look

  • 5,110 records; 249 stroke cases in the full dataset
  • Four classifiers compared against an imbalanced test set
  • Recorded decision tree: 11 of 62 stroke cases found; 40 false alarms
  • README explains why three models found no true stroke cases

Machine-learning study

Warranty claim fraud analysis

The problem
Can claim data distinguish fraudulent warranty requests from genuine ones?
My contribution
Explored claim patterns, compared three classifiers and checked the majority baseline.
The result
The recorded tree found 1 of 8 test frauds; the other models found none. The study documents why accuracy was misleading.
Pythonscikit-learnFraud analysisClass imbalance
Explore project

A study of 358 warranty claims for air conditioners and televisions. It combines exploratory analysis with decision-tree, random-forest and logistic-regression models. The report documents poor fraud recall despite 91–92% accuracy, a small test set and an outlier filter that removed fraud examples. Dataset provenance is incomplete; findings are exploratory and do not establish a production fraud-detection system.

A closer look

  • 358 claims; 35 labelled fraud cases
  • Comparison of claim value, product age and purchase channel
  • Recorded decision tree: 1 of 8 test frauds detected; 2 false alarms
  • Random forest and logistic regression matched the all-genuine baseline

  • README.md (MD)
