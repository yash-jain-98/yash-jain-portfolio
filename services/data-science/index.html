BEFORE WE START

Clear scope.
Useful expectations.

What to bring

Describe the question, the available dataset, how it was collected and any known restrictions. We first assess whether the data is suitable for the proposed analysis.

How the project is scoped

These services focus on analysis, prototypes and documented studies. Production deployment and ongoing model monitoring require separate scoping. Portfolio models are demonstrations, not guarantees of predictive performance on your business data.

A few questions,
answered.

How is data science different from dashboard reporting?

Reporting primarily describes what happened using agreed measures. A modelling study can investigate patterns or estimate an outcome, but requires evaluation and may not be suitable for every dataset.

Can you guarantee a prediction accuracy?

No. Results depend on data quality, the problem and the evaluation setup. We agree relevant measures and report the observed results and limitations honestly.

Are the portfolio models deployed business systems?

The data-science portfolio contains project studies, notebooks and coursework. Each project identifies its context; it should not be treated as evidence of a production deployment.

Can we start with a small study?

Yes. A focused exploratory analysis or baseline model can help establish whether the question and available data justify further work.
