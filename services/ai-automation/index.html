How the project is scoped

The quote depends on the number of connected tools, workflow branches, AI steps and testing required. Platform subscriptions, API usage and ongoing support are discussed separately. Access to the necessary accounts or APIs must be available.

A few questions,
answered.

Can you automate work in Gmail and Google Sheets?

Yes. My portfolio includes email classification with attachment filing, lead matching across sheets and email summaries in Google Docs. We first check your account access and the exact workflow you need.

Does every automation need AI?

No. A rule-based script or Make.com scenario is often enough. AI is useful where text needs interpretation, but its output still needs appropriate checks.

Can you connect Google Sheets to WhatsApp?

My portfolio includes a Sheets-triggered messaging workflow with a 12-hour follow-up. The setup for your business depends on the messaging provider, account permissions and applicable message-template requirements.

What happens if an automation fails?

We agree the error handling and review process as part of the scope. That can include a log, an alert or a manual review queue. Ongoing monitoring is scoped separately.
