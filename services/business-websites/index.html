BEFORE WE START

Clear scope.
Useful expectations.

What to bring

Share your website, target locations, services or products, customer profile and existing Google or Meta accounts. For advertising, include your intended monthly media budget and any brand or creative assets.

How the project is scoped

Services can be booked individually or together. Website development, campaign management, profile maintenance and ongoing search work are scoped separately. Advertising spend is paid to the platforms and is separate from service fees. Hosting, subscriptions and creative production are agreed in advance. Google controls profile verification; search rankings, AI citations and advertising returns cannot be guaranteed.

A few questions,
answered.

Can I book marketing without a new website?

Yes. Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Business Profile management and search optimisation can be scoped for an existing website. We first check whether the landing pages and enquiry journeys support the intended campaign.

Do you manage Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Yes. The service can include setup, targeting, campaign management, agreed creative testing, tracking and reporting. We agree the goals, scope and review schedule before launch. Platform advertising spend is separate from management fees.

Can you set up and maintain my Google Business Profile?

Yes, for eligible businesses. I can help with setup, verification steps, business details, categories, services and ongoing updates. Verification and profile approval remain with Google; maintaining a profile does not guarantee a particular local ranking.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on discovery through search engines. AEO focuses on content that directly answers customer questions. GEO focuses on helping generative AI systems understand and reference useful content. They overlap through clear structure, accurate business information and credible content. No service can guarantee inclusion in an AI answer.
