BEFORE WE START

Clear scope.
Useful expectations.

What to bring

Share your current website if you have one, a short description of your customers, the pages or products you need and any brand assets. A few examples of sites you like help clarify the direction.

How the project is scoped

Page count, content readiness, ecommerce features and integrations determine the scope and timeline. Hosting, domains, paid themes, plugins and maintenance are discussed before work starts. Search visibility grows through ongoing content and SEO work; a new website does not guarantee rankings.

A few questions,
answered.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Responsive layouts, readable text, navigation and the main customer journeys are part of the website build and testing.

Do you use WordPress, Shopify or custom code?

The choice depends on the job. We discuss your content editing needs, catalogue, integrations and budget before choosing the platform.

Can you work with businesses in Australia?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in India and Australia. Australian Boat Parties is included in my live website portfolio. We agree communication times and review milestones at the start.

Can you improve a website I already have?

Yes. Share the site and the problems you want to solve. We can scope a focused improvement or a broader redesign after reviewing the current setup.
